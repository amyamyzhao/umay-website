How to Choose a Fur Garment Factory in China: MOQ, Sampling, QC Checklist

Eight things every buyer should verify before placing an order — from factory verification and honest MOQ to QC process, certifications, lead times and red flags.

By Jiaxing Umay Garment Co., Ltd. · Updated August 2026 · 4-minute read

Quick answer

China's fur garment industry is concentrated in a few clusters — Jiaxing, Zhejiang (where many fur and leather coat factories are based), Hebei and Guangdong. Working with the right factory is the difference between a smooth season and a disaster.

This checklist covers the eight things every buyer should verify before placing an order.

01 · Verify

Confirm the factory physically exists and is independently verified before you pay anything.

02 · Sample first

Request a pre-production sample and approve material, color, measurements and workmanship before committing to bulk.

03 · QC in writing

Get the QC process, inspection stages and pre-shipment photo/video policy confirmed in writing.

1. Verify the Factory Actually Exists

  • Platform verification: On Alibaba.com, look for Onsite Check (third-party verification), Trade Assurance, and the company's registered business type.
  • Ask for proof: Business license (redacted), factory floor photos or a live video call, and their physical address in Jiaxing or the relevant cluster.
  • Cross-check the website: The company name, address, and contact details should match across the independent site, the Alibaba store and LinkedIn.
Tip: A factory that is Alibaba Onsite-Checked has had a third-party inspector physically visit and verify the premises. This is the fastest proxy for legitimacy.

2. Understand MOQ Honestly

Beware of "any MOQ" promises. Ask the factory to state:

  • Sample MOQ — can you order 1 sample piece before bulk?
  • Bulk MOQ — the real minimum for the selected material, color, size mix, labels and design changes; never rely on a site-wide headline.
  • Mixed-model orders — can you combine several designs into one bulk order to reach the MOQ?
Important: Get the MOQ in writing in the PI (proforma invoice). A factory that changes MOQ after you start is a red flag.

3. Sampling: Speed and Refund Policy

  • Sample lead time: ask for a dated schedule after material availability and the complete tech pack are reviewed.
  • Sample fee: Most factories charge a sample fee (refundable against your first bulk order). Ask how many pieces trigger the refund (e.g., over 200 pcs).
  • Photo/video updates: A reliable factory sends real-time photos or videos during sampling, not just a final photo.

4. Quality Control Process

Ask the factory to walk you through their QC steps. A serious factory should describe a process like:

  1. Raw material inspection (fur quality, leather grade, fabric)
  2. Fur/fabric processing and preparation
  3. Cutting and sewing
  4. In-process quality checks
  5. Finished-product inspection (every piece hand-inspected)
  6. Packing and export carton check
Key question to ask: Do you provide pre-shipment photos/videos of my actual order? (Online QC) — this single step prevents most disputes.

5. Certifications and Compliance

  • Factory audits: ISO9001, BSCI/SEDEX social compliance audits.
  • Material tests: OEKO-TEX or equivalent for faux fur fabrics; leather/fur origin documents where relevant.
  • Export documents: For real fur, confirm HS codes (4303.10 for fur apparel), CITES permits where required, and that the factory has experience shipping to your country.
  • Labeling: The factory should support accurate fiber/fur content labels (EU 1007/2011, US Fur Products Labeling Act).

7. Communication

  • Language: English communication should be clear; WhatsApp response time (24h or better) is a good indicator.
  • One contact person: A dedicated account manager makes the process smoother and avoids information getting lost between departments.
  • Written quotes: Everything — price, MOQ, lead time, shipping — in a written quote or PI. Verbal agreements are not sufficient.

8. Red Flags to Avoid

Walk away — or ask very specific questions — if you see any of the following:

  • No physical address or refuses a video call.
  • MOQ, lead time or price changes after you commit.
  • Unverifiable claims ("1000+ clients", "99% satisfaction") with no supporting evidence.
  • Refuses to provide pre-shipment QC photos of your actual order.
  • Labels real fur as faux, or faux as real — a compliance and legal risk for you.
Compliance risk: Mislabeling fur type or origin is not just unethical — it can result in customs seizure, fines and reputational damage in your market.

FAQ

What is a normal MOQ for fur garments from China?

There is no universal industry MOQ. UMAY starts from 1 piece per style for samples or production orders, while material availability, color and construction still need written confirmation.

Can I get a sample before bulk production?

Yes. UMAY sampling starts from 1 piece per style; timing and charges are confirmed after the material, construction, measurements and color specification is reviewed.

How do I verify a factory on Alibaba?

Check for the Onsite Check badge (third-party verification), Trade Assurance, business type, and years on the platform. Then ask for a video call and factory photos.
