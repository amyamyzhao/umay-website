How to Choose a Fur Garment Factory in China: MOQ, Sampling, QC Checklist

Eight things every buyer should verify before placing an order — from factory verification and honest MOQ to QC process, certifications, lead times and red flags.

By Jiaxing Umay Garment Co., Ltd. · Reviewed by Zoey, Sales Manager · More than 10 years of experience working with fur coat projects · Updated September 2026 · 4-minute read

Quick answer

Start by identifying the supplier’s actual role in your order: direct manufacturer, trading company or a hybrid model. Verify the registered business, production responsibility, material sourcing, quality control access and the party named in the quotation and agreement.

This checklist helps a buyer compare responsibilities and evidence before commercial commitment.

Factory, Trading Company or Hybrid Supplier?

These labels describe different operating models, but the buyer still needs to verify who signs the agreement, who controls production, who sources materials and who is responsible for quality and corrective action.

1. Verify Identity and Production Responsibility

  • Platform information: Treat badges and profile fields as verification leads; check their current scope, the registered business identity and the represented premises independently.
  • Ask for evidence: Request an appropriate business-license copy, the represented production address, current production views or a live video call, and a clear explanation of any subcontracted processes.
  • Cross-check transaction details: Compare the company name and responsibility stated on the website, written quotation, proforma invoice, agreement and payment instructions. Resolve inconsistencies before payment.
Tip: Treat platform badges as leads, not a complete identity or production-responsibility check. Verify their current scope, the business identity and the represented premises independently.

2. Understand MOQ Honestly

Beware of "any MOQ" promises. Ask the factory to state:

  • Sample MOQ — ask whether a separate sample MOQ applies and confirm the sample scope, charge and credit conditions in writing.
  • Bulk MOQ — the real minimum for the selected material, color, size mix, labels and design changes; never rely on a site-wide headline.
  • Mixed-model orders — can you combine several designs into one bulk order to reach the MOQ?
Important: Get the MOQ in writing in the PI (proforma invoice). A factory that changes MOQ after you start is a red flag.

3. Sampling: Scope, Credit and Payment Terms

  • Sample lead time: ask for a dated schedule after material availability and the complete tech pack are reviewed.
  • Sample quotation: UMAY’s sample quotation includes the sample garment and shipping.
  • Pre-sample pattern adjustment: Pattern adjustments agreed before sample making begins carry no adjustment charge.
  • Bulk-order credit: The paid sample fee is credited toward the subsequent bulk order.
  • Payment terms: UMAY supports 50% deposit and 50% balance; the written quotation records when the balance becomes due.
  • Photo/video evidence: UMAY provides garment photos, videos and photos showing measurements for buyer review.

4. Quality Control Process

Ask the supplier to document the QC steps proposed for the exact order. The written plan can include:

  1. Raw material inspection (fur quality, leather grade, fabric)
  2. Fur/fabric processing and preparation
  3. Cutting and sewing
  4. In-process quality checks
  5. Finished-product inspection scope and sampling method defined in writing
  6. Packing and export carton check
UMAY inspection support: Buyers may arrange their own inspection. UMAY also provides garment photos, videos and photos showing measurements; the requested checkpoints and timing are recorded with the order. Wholesale buyers also receive social-media promotional images for the agreed project.

5. Certifications and Compliance

  • Certificates and audits: Verify the certificate owner, scope, issuer, standard and validity; do not infer certification from a logo.
  • Material tests: Define the required method, sample scope, laboratory and acceptance criterion for the selected material and destination.
  • Classification and documents: Confirm the applicable classification, permits and destination documents with a licensed customs broker or qualified compliance adviser.
  • Labeling: Approve the final composition, origin, care and other required wording with the importer or qualified adviser for the destination.

7. Communication

  • Communication: Agree the working language, communication channel, responsible contact and response expectations for the project.
  • Named responsibility: Record the commercial contact, technical contact, approval channel and person responsible for production updates.
  • Written quotes: Everything — price, MOQ, lead time, shipping — in a written quote or PI. Verbal agreements are not sufficient.

8. Red Flags to Avoid

Investigate and resolve the following points before commercial commitment:

  • No physical address or refuses a video call.
  • MOQ, lead time or price changes after you commit.
  • Unverifiable claims ("1000+ clients", "99% satisfaction") with no supporting evidence.
  • Refuses to provide pre-shipment QC photos of your actual order.
  • Labels real fur as faux, or faux as real — a compliance and legal risk for you.
Compliance risk: Material identity, origin and label wording must match the approved records and destination requirements. Ask a qualified customs or compliance adviser about the rules for the target market.

FAQ

What is a normal MOQ for fur garments from China?

There is no universal industry MOQ. UMAY confirms MOQ in writing after reviewing the selected style, material, color direction, quantity and customization scope, while material availability, color and construction still need written confirmation.

Can I get a sample before bulk production?

Yes. UMAY’s sample quotation includes the sample garment and shipping. Pattern adjustments agreed before sample making begins carry no adjustment charge, and the paid sample fee is credited toward the subsequent bulk order. Timing and the remaining transaction details are confirmed in writing.

How do I verify a factory on Alibaba?

Treat platform badges and profile fields as leads. Verify their current scope, the registered business identity, the represented premises and production responsibility independently before payment.
