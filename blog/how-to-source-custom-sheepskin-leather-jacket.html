Overview

Sourcing a custom sheepskin leather jacket from a China OEM factory involves six steps: defining your spec, vetting factories, confirming MOQ and price, sampling, bulk approval, and private label setup. Done right, you can have a confirmed sample in hand within a week and bulk production started within a month.

The 6-Step Sourcing Process

1

Define Your Product Specification

Before contacting any factory, prepare a basic spec sheet. You do not need a technical pack at this stage — a clear brief is enough to get an accurate quote. Include: style reference image or sketch, target silhouette (biker, moto, bomber, long coat), color or Pantone reference, size range (S–XL, XS–3XL, etc.), lining preference, hardware notes, and any branding requirements (label, hang tag, packaging).

The more complete your brief, the fewer back-and-forth rounds before sampling. Factories that ask zero questions before quoting are a red flag.

2

Find and Vet a Sheepskin Leather Jacket Factory

Focus on factories based in established leather garment hubs — Jiaxing and Haining in Zhejiang province are two of the most concentrated clusters for sheepskin and fur garment production in China. Look for:

  • Verifiable production photos (not stock images)
  • Stated MOQ policy in writing — not vague "negotiable"
  • Clear sample policy — fee amount, lead time, revision rounds
  • Category experience matching your target style
  • Willingness to share material swatches before sampling

Avoid factories that promise unrealistically low MOQ with no sample fee — these often can't deliver consistent quality at bulk scale.

3

Confirm MOQ, Unit Price and Payment Terms

Get pricing confirmed in writing before moving to sampling. Key figures to pin down:

  • Sample quotation: request a quotation based on your material selection, specifications and planned order quantity; confirm any credit terms in writing before sampling
  • Bulk MOQ: at UMAY, MOQ is 1 piece — confirm per style and colorway
  • Unit price: depends on material grade, hardware, and quantity tier
  • Payment terms: standard is 30% deposit on order confirmation, 70% before shipment
  • Lead time: bulk production typically 30–45 days after sample approval
4

Request and Review a Physical Sample

Never approve a bulk order based on photos alone. A physical sample lets you assess hide quality, stitching consistency, hardware finish, and overall construction. When reviewing your sample, check:

  • Hide feel and surface grain — does it match the grade you specified?
  • Stitch density and thread color consistency
  • Zipper and hardware operation — no snagging, smooth pull
  • Lining attachment — no loose edges or puckering
  • Color accuracy against your reference
  • Measurements — compare to your size spec at all key points

Request one revision round if needed. Document all feedback in writing with photos — this becomes part of your production spec.

5

Approve Sample and Place Bulk Order

Once the sample is approved, confirm the production order in writing. Your order confirmation should include: approved sample reference, quantity by size and color, unit price, total order value, payment schedule, production lead time, and shipping method. Keep the approved physical sample — it is your QC reference at shipment inspection.

6

Set Up Private Label Branding

For a private label run, provide your brand assets before production starts: woven label artwork (brand name, size, care symbols), hang tag design and copy, and packaging spec (poly bag, box, tissue). Most factories can produce or source these components locally — confirm lead time and cost before the bulk order is placed, not after.

At UMAY, private label setup (label, hang tag, poly bag) is handled as part of the OEM order. Minimum quantities for custom labels vary — confirm per item.

Pre-Order Checklist — Confirm Before Sampling

  • Style reference image or sketch provided
  • Color reference confirmed (Pantone or physical swatch)
  • Size range and key measurements specified
  • Material grade confirmed — sheepskin type, lining, hardware
  • Sample fee amount and refund policy confirmed in writing
  • Sample lead time confirmed in writing
  • Bulk MOQ confirmed per style and colorway
  • Revision policy confirmed — number of rounds included
  • Private label requirements communicated
  • Payment terms agreed and documented

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MOQ for custom sheepskin leather jackets from a China factory?

MOQ varies by factory. At UMAY Garment, the MOQ is 1 piece — this applies to both samples and bulk orders. Some factories require 50–200 pieces minimum for custom styles. Always confirm MOQ per style and colorway before requesting a sample.

How long does it take to get a leather jacket sample from a China OEM factory?

At UMAY, sample lead time is 3 days from the date your spec is confirmed and sample payment is received. International shipping (DHL or FedEx) typically adds 3–5 business days depending on your location.

How do I find a reliable sheepskin leather jacket manufacturer in China?

Look for factories with verifiable production photos, clear MOQ and sample policies in writing, experience with your target style, and willingness to provide material references before sampling. Jiaxing and Haining are established leather garment manufacturing hubs in China.

Can I order a custom sheepskin leather jacket with my own label?

Yes. At UMAY, private label setup — including woven brand label, hang tag, and poly bag — is handled as part of the OEM order. Provide your brand assets before production starts so labels are ready when garments are finished.
