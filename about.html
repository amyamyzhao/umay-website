Who We Are

Jiaxing Umay Garment Co., Ltd.

Jiaxing Umay Garment Co., Ltd. is located in Jiaxing, Zhejiang, China and supplies custom fur, leather, shearling and faux-fur outerwear. The website provides product evidence, sourcing guides and direct contact for written B2B specifications.

UMAY works with fashion brands, boutiques, wholesalers and designers on photographed reference styles and buyer-supplied designs. Each project is confirmed through material, construction, measurements, color, labeling, packaging and quality-control checkpoints.
