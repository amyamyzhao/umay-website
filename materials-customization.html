Direct Buyer Answers

Materials & Customization FAQ

What measurements should I send for custom fur or leather outerwear?

Send bust, sleeve length and back length as the starting measurements. If your design needs more control, add shoulder, waist, hip, garment length and ease requirements.

Can I provide a finished-garment size chart?

Yes. A brand or finished-garment size chart is welcome. The intended fit and measurement tolerance should be confirmed before the sample is approved.

Are the 44 colors available for every material?

No. The 44-color reference is mainly for fox fur. Faux-fur colors can be developed from a Pantone reference or physical swatch, subject to sample and batch approval.

Can labels and packaging be customized from one piece?

Yes. Projects can start from 1 piece per style, with main labels, care labels, hang tags and packaging confirmed in the written specification.
