Direct Buyer Answers

OEM Faux-Fur Project FAQ

These answers explain the project process without presenting a fixed retail price, stock promise or universal MOQ.

How do I request a quote for an OEM faux-fur coat?

Choose a photographed style or send your own reference image, sketch, sample or tech pack. Include the requested material direction, measurements, color, lining, closures, labels, packaging, quantity and destination. UMAY reviews the brief and confirms the feasible scope and quotation in writing.

Which parts of a faux-fur coat or jacket can be customized?

Requests can cover the material direction, color or print, finished measurements, fit, lining, closures, hardware, labels and packaging. Feasibility is reviewed against the selected construction and material before the approved scope is recorded.

Why is there no fixed price on this page?

These are B2B development references rather than fixed-stock retail items. A quotation depends on the selected style, material, construction, measurements, colorway, branding, packaging, quantity, destination and agreed commercial terms.

How is MOQ confirmed?

MOQ is reviewed by project after the style, material, colorway, quantity and customization scope are known. Sample quantity and production MOQ should be stated as separate written terms.

What should be approved before bulk production?

Approve the selected material and color reference, finished measurements and tolerances, garment construction, physical sample, labels and packaging, order quantity, inspection basis and agreed schedule in writing.

Can buyers arrange inspection and receive project images?

Yes. UMAY supports buyer-arranged inspection. UMAY also provides garment photos, videos and photos showing measurements for buyer review. Wholesale buyers also receive social-media promotional images for the agreed project; timing and requested views are recorded with the order.
