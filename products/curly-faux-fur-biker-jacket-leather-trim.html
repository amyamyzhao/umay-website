MOQ, Custom Fit & Private Label

Is the black trim real leather?

Yes. The trim on the photographed reference is genuine leather; the main body is curly faux fur.

Can the faux-fur color be customized?

The photographed colors are references only. Submit an alternate color direction, Pantone reference or physical swatch for feasibility review; any approved direction is recorded in the written project specification.

How is MOQ confirmed for this jacket?

MOQ is not fixed across styles. It is confirmed in writing for each project after the selected style, material, color direction, quantity and customization scope are reviewed. Sizing inputs are reviewed and confirmed in the written project specification.

Can I provide my own jacket design?

Yes. Send a reference image, sketch, physical sample or tech pack, plus measurements, color, labels, packaging and target quantity.
