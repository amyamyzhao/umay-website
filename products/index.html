Quick Buyer Answers

Custom Outerwear Development FAQ

How do I compare custom outerwear development options?

Use the five core category hubs, then review each photographed style for its measurements, material notes, private-label scope and written order terms.

Can a supplier review my reference image or tech pack?

Send a reference image, sketch, physical sample or tech pack for feasibility review. The accepted development scope is confirmed in writing.

What details help with a private-label quotation?

Share the material direction, measurements or size chart, colorways, branding placements, packaging, quantity and destination.

Can I customize an existing fur or leather jacket style?

Yes. Start from a photographed reference and tailor the fit, color, lining, closure, labels and packaging for your brand.

Don't See Your Style?

Send Cathy your reference image, sketch, physical sample or tech pack. We will review the material, construction, measurements and target quantity for a tailored quotation.
