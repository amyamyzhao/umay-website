RFQ Inputs

Before You Send an Inquiry

A complete brief gets you a faster, more accurate quote. Include these points with your first message — no need to be perfect, we will help you fill the gaps:

① Style & Reference

Reference photos, sketches or a tech pack of the coat/vest design you want (length, collar, closure, pockets).

② Quantity & Measurements

Planned quantity plus body measurements, finished-garment measurements or your own size chart.

③ Color & Material

Fox type preference (silver / arctic / red), natural or dyed, and target color (Pantone code or photo).

④ Market & Files

Destination country (affects compliance and shipping) and any label/packaging/document requirements.

Buyer Guide

How to Compare Fox Fur Suppliers

Before comparing quotes, confirm that each supplier quotes on the same specification — fox type, length, lining and dye process. Ask each one to state their sample policy, pelt-grade evidence, QC checkpoints and delivery terms; a lower quote often means a different construction. Because fox fur is a real animal fur, also verify the import rules for your destination market before ordering.
