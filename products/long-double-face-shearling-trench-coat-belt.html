Buyer Questions

MOQ, Custom Fit & Private Label

What is the MOQ for this shearling trench coat?

The minimum order is a project-specific MOQ. Custom measurements, colors, labels and packaging are available from the same starting quantity.

What does double-face shearling mean?

The photographed construction uses sheepskin with the fur and leather sides of the same hide, rather than a separate faux-fur layer.

Can the coat be made to custom measurements?

Yes. Provide body measurements, finished-garment measurements or your brand size chart for pattern and fit review.

Can I change the color, buttons or belt?

Yes. Approve the color with a physical swatch and confirm button, belt, pocket and label details on the sample.
