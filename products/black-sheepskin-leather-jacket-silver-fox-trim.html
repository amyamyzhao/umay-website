MOQ, Custom Fit & Private Label

Is the whole jacket made of silver fox fur?

No. The body is sheepskin leather and the oversized collar, front edge and cuffs use silver fox fur.

Can the leather and fur colors be changed?

Yes. Approve the leather finish and fur-color direction with physical materials or a garment sample before production.

Can this jacket be made to custom measurements?

Yes. Send bust, sleeve length and back length, or provide a finished-garment size chart.

What is the private-label MOQ?

MOQ is confirmed after style, material, quantity and branding review; approved labels and packaging are recorded in the written project specification.
