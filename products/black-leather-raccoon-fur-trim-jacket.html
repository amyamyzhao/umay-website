Buyer Questions

Low MOQ, Custom Fit & Private Label FAQ

Direct answers to the questions buyers most often ask when looking for a custom fur coat manufacturer or private-label outerwear supplier.

What is the MOQ for this Leather Jacket with Raccoon Fur Trim?

The minimum order is 1 piece. Share the intended measurements, color, branding and packaging direction for a tailored quotation.

Can you make it to custom measurements?

Yes. Send body measurements, garment measurements or your brand size chart. The fit can be developed around your target customer rather than a fixed retail size.

Can I add private labels and custom packaging?

Yes. Discuss logo placement, main labels, care labels, hang tags and packaging while the garment details are being prepared.

Can I send my own design or tech pack?

Yes. A reference image, sketch, sample or tech pack can be used to discuss material sourcing, pattern development, custom cut-and-sew construction and sampling.

What should I send for a useful quotation?

Include the target material, measurements or size chart, colorways, construction details, branding placements, packaging, quantity and destination. Clear specifications reduce back-and-forth during sample development.
