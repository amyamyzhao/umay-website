Quick buyer answers

Before you begin

Is the pink body material confirmed?

No fixed body substrate or fiber composition is assigned on this page. Share the body material and hand-feel direction you need so it can be confirmed in the written project specification.

Can I use standard sizes or request custom measurements?

Send the intended size labels together with target finished-garment measurements or a brand size chart. Feasibility and grading are confirmed in writing for the selected style and material. Displayed labels do not indicate stock or guaranteed availability.

Which customization requests can be reviewed?

Labels, packaging, lining, hardware and other customization requests can be reviewed. The approved scope is confirmed in writing for the selected project. MOQ and any structural-change threshold are confirmed after the selected style and construction are reviewed.

When can a sample be ready?

The sample target date and production schedule are confirmed in writing after style, material, quantity and specification review. The agreed start condition is recorded for the selected project.

How should this fur-trimmed garment be cared for?

Home washing or machine washing is not recommended. If cleaning is needed, consult a professional fur-garment care specialist and follow the instructions agreed for the actual garment materials.
