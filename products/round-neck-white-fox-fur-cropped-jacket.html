Direct buyer answers

Buyer questions

Does white color identify the fox species?

No. Color is not species evidence. The formal species must come from supplier records or the approved material sample.

How is the white color direction specified?

The approved sample and color record specify whether the direction is natural white, bleached or dyed.

Is this the same as another white or blue fox short jacket?

The round-neck structure is distinct from the hooded and large-collar groups reviewed locally. A final paper-pattern and species check against the existing blue-fox page is still required.

What should the project specification include?

Include the formal species, color treatment, complete measurements, closure and lining, confirmed against the approved sample and written order specification.
