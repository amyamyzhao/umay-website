Quick buyer answers

Before you begin

Are the grey and green references offered under one style code?

Yes. Both photographed color directions are grouped under UMS-LTFX-010. Confirm the selected color standard, trim, lining and other project details in writing before sample approval.

What material information is confirmed?

The confirmed direction is fox fur outerwear. The body base material, lining, exact composition percentages and finish are selected and recorded for the individual project.

Can I use standard sizes or request custom measurements?

Send the target finished-garment measurements or brand size chart and the color direction. Feasibility and available options are reviewed for the selected style and material and confirmed in writing. Displayed size labels are references only; they do not indicate stock or guaranteed availability.

Which customization requests can be reviewed?

Labels, packaging, lining, hardware and other customization requests can be reviewed. The approved scope is confirmed in writing for the selected project. MOQ and any structural-change threshold are confirmed after the selected style and construction are reviewed.

When can a sample be ready?

Sample and production target dates are confirmed in writing after the selected style, material, color direction, quantity, specification completeness and revision scope are reviewed.

How should this garment be cared for?

Home washing or machine washing is not recommended. If cleaning is needed, consult a professional fur-care specialist and follow the instructions agreed for the actual garment materials.
