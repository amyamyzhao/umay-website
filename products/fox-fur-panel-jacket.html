Quick buyer answers

Before you begin

Are the grey and green references offered under one style code?

Yes. Both photographed color directions are grouped under UMS-LTFX-010. Confirm the selected color standard, trim, lining and other project details in writing before sample approval.

What material information is confirmed?

The confirmed direction is fox fur outerwear. The body base material, lining, exact composition percentages and finish are selected and recorded for the individual project.

Can I use standard sizes or request custom measurements?

Yes. Standard size labels such as S, M, L and XL can be used, and custom measurements are available. The labels are references, not a fixed finished-garment measurement chart or an in-stock size range.

What can I customize from one piece?

Color, sizing, body material, fur, lining, labels and packaging can be customized from 1 piece. Collar or sleeve structure changes and hardware customization require 50 pieces. Minimum quantities are counted across the whole order.

When can a sample be ready?

The stated sample lead time is 3 days after the deposit is received. Bulk production timing depends on quantity and is counted from receipt of the deposit; discuss the schedule for your order.

How should this garment be cared for?

Home washing or machine washing is not recommended. If cleaning is needed, consult a professional fur-care specialist and follow the instructions agreed for the actual garment materials.
