MOQ, Custom Fit & Private Label

What does double-face shearling mean here?

The body uses a sheepskin/shearling direction with leather and wool sides of the hide; the oversized curly trim is specified separately.

Can the body and collar colors be mixed?

Yes. Approve both materials and their color pairing on the sample before production.

Can the jacket be made to custom measurements?

Yes. Send bust, sleeve length and back length, or a finished-garment size chart.

How are MOQ, labels and packaging confirmed?

MOQ, label and packaging requirements are confirmed after the selected style, materials, quantity and branding details are reviewed.
