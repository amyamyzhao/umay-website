Buyer questions

Color-series decisions

Why are the nine colors on one page?

They share the same confirmed paper pattern and silhouette. One page avoids repetitive search results while making color comparison easier.

Are the colors guaranteed to match a screen?

Photography is a direction. Approve the selected material and color reference under agreed lighting before sampling or production.

What should be confirmed for fit?

Send your finished-garment measurements or brand size chart, then confirm bust, shoulder, sleeve and back length against the approved sample.

Choose a color and send your specification

Include the SKU, size chart or target measurements, quantity, labels, packaging and destination.
