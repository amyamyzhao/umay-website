MOQ, Custom Fit & Private Label

What leather species is used?

The supplied reference identifies genuine leather but does not confirm the species, so cowhide or sheepskin should be selected and written into the project specification.

Where is the fox fur placed?

Real fox fur is positioned around the shoulders and upper sleeves; the body and remaining sleeves are leather.

Can the orange color be changed?

Yes. Approve the leather and fox-fur color pair using physical samples.

What is the MOQ?

MOQ is confirmed in writing after style and material review with custom measurements, hardware and branding available.
