Quick buyer answers

Before you begin

Can I use standard sizes or request custom measurements?

Yes. S, M, L and XL can be used as standard size labels, and custom measurements are available. The labels are references, not a fixed finished-garment measurement chart or an in-stock size range.

What can I customize from one piece?

Color, sizing, body material, fur, lining, labels and packaging can be customized from 1 piece. Collar or sleeve structure changes and hardware customization require 50 pieces. Minimum quantities are counted across the whole order.

When can a sample be ready?

The stated sample lead time is 3 days after the deposit is received. Bulk production timing depends on quantity and is counted from receipt of the deposit.

How should this leather-and-fur garment be cared for?

Home washing or machine washing is not recommended. If cleaning is needed, consult a professional leather-and-fur care specialist and follow the instructions agreed for the actual garment materials.
