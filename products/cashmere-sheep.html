Material Boundary

Do Not Treat Every Wool-Like Surface as the Same Material

Double-face shearling

Use this term only for a style whose project record confirms the construction. It describes a selected hide construction, not every curly-lamb or wool-look garment on the site.

Curly-lamb body or trim

Record the body and trim separately. Photographs can show placement and surface direction but do not establish hidden construction, composition percentages or labeling claims.

Composition by selected style

The photographs show double-face shearling and curly-lamb directions. Exact body, trim, lining and fibre or hide composition are confirmed by selected style in writing.
