Buyer questions

How is the exact fur species confirmed?

The formal species and long-pile cuff material are confirmed from the product label, supplier records and approved sample—not inferred from photography.

How should a multicolor layout be approved?

Define every color-block position in the tech pack and approve physical material swatches or a finished sample before production.

Which measurements define this style?

Use bust, shoulder, sleeve length, sleeve width, cuff, back length and hem measurements in the project specification.
